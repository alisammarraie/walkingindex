    HOW TO READ THIS

    Access is a connection.

    Shortest paths are calculated from your selected junction. The green paths include the reachable part of a segment, even when its far end lies beyond your time budget.

    Destination points attach to the nearest path vertex within 100 m. The connector distance is included; its physical accessibility is not verified.
